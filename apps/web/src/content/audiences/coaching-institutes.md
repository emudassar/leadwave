## Admission season is a volume problem

The same six questions, several hundred times, in a window of a few weeks: fees,
batch timings, syllabus, demo class, location, results. Your counsellors spend
the season typing instead of counselling.

## Automate the six

One keyword automation each. That is most of the inbound volume handled inside a
second, at any hour, without a counsellor touching it.

## Capture the number, not just the click

For an admissions enquiry the phone number is the asset. Ask for it in the same
conversation — Messenger offers it as a one-tap chip — and it lands in your
contacts, normalised, ready for the counselling team to work through.

## Language

Parents write in English, Hindi, Urdu and Roman transliterations of both, often
in the same message. If you turn on LeadWave AI, its default is to reply in
whichever language the person wrote in, so nobody gets an English wall of text in
reply to a Roman Urdu question.

## Leave the decision to a counsellor

Scholarship eligibility, fee concessions, whether a particular student should
join a particular batch — all of that belongs to a person. Put those words on the
exclusion list and let the automation do the logistics it is good at.
