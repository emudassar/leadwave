## The reporting problem

Affiliate dashboards tell you about conversions. They tell you nothing useful
about the top of the funnel — how many people you actually sent, from which
post, on which day. So you optimise blind.

## One keyword per offer

Set up a separate automation for each offer, each with its own keyword and its
own link. *TOOL* sends one, *COURSE* sends another. Every link is minted as a
tracked short link when you publish, so clicks are attributed per automation
without you building anything.

## Or one carousel for several

A product carousel puts up to ten offers in one message, each card with its own
link and its own click count. That is how you find out which of your five
recommendations people actually tap, rather than guessing from the one that
happened to convert.

## Say it is an affiliate link

Put the disclosure in the message itself, not in a bio nobody reads. It is
required in most jurisdictions, it costs you almost nothing in conversion, and
the alternative is a platform problem you do not want.

## Keep the exclusions tight

Add *scam*, *refund* and *is this legit* to the exclusion list on every
automation. Those messages deserve a person, and an automated affiliate link in
reply to them is the fastest way to earn a report.
