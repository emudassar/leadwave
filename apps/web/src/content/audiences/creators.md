## The reach you already have

You do not have a traffic problem. A post does well, forty people comment, and
thirty-two of them never hear back because you were filming the next one. That
gap is the entire opportunity, and it closes on its own within a day.

## The first automation to set up

**Comment → private message.** Pick the keyword you already tell people to
comment — *link*, *price*, *guide* — and write the message they should get. The
public reply goes under the comment so the rest of the comments section sees you
answering, and the real thing lands in their inbox.

That one rule handles the post that blows up at 2am, which is the one you would
otherwise wake up to and give up on.

## Then the two that compound

**Story replies.** Every reply and reaction to a Page story becomes a thread. It
is the lowest-effort engagement you get, and almost nobody automates it.

**A follow-up nudge.** One reminder to the people who never opened the link.
Not a sequence — one. It cancels itself the moment they reply or tap.

## What you learn that you cannot see now

Facebook tells you a post got reach. LeadWave tells you it sent 212 people to
your link and 41 different people opened it. Run that twice and you know which
kind of post actually moves people, rather than which one got likes.

## The honest part

The Follow Gate asks for a follow and unlocks on a tap. It does not verify the
follow — Facebook gives apps no per-person follow signal, so anyone claiming
otherwise is counting taps and calling them follows. We count taps and call them
taps, labelled self-confirmed everywhere.
