## Launch day is the whole month

Most of a digital product's revenue arrives in the first forty-eight hours, and
most of that traffic arrives as comments on one post. Answering them by hand is
the bottleneck, and it is a bottleneck at exactly the wrong moment.

## Set it up before you post

**Comment → checkout.** One keyword, one message, the checkout link in a button.
Every commenter gets it within seconds — including the ones who comment while
you are asleep on the other side of the launch.

**Then ask for the email.** They have the link; ask whether they want the free
chapter too. One tap sends their profile email straight into your contacts,
attached to the post that earned it. That list is what makes launch two easier
than launch one.

**Then nudge once.** Ninety minutes later, anyone who never opened the checkout
gets a single reminder with the link again. It cancels itself for anyone who
already bought.

## The number that actually helps

A tracked short link separates 612 clicks from 441 different people. On a launch
those are very different numbers: the first tells you about enthusiasm, the
second tells you about your conversion rate.

## Where it stops

LeadWave sends the link and counts the click. It does not process the payment,
and it cannot tell you who bought — that lives in Gumroad or Stan. What it gives
you is the top of that funnel, measured properly for the first time.
