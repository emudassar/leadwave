## You are working, not watching your phone

That is the whole problem. Enquiries arrive during appointments — which is
exactly when you cannot answer them, and exactly when they are most likely to
message the next salon on their list instead.

## The one automation that matters

Comment or message *price*, *rate* or the name of a treatment, and they get the
price list plus a booking button within seconds, while they are still thinking
about it. The click on that button is counted, so you can tell which post
actually fills the diary.

## Filling a gap this afternoon

Post the cancellation to your Page story. Every reply to that story triggers an
automation with the slot and the booking link. It is the fastest route from "we
have a 4pm free" to someone in the chair, and it needs no effort on the day.

## Follow up the ones who did not book

Turn on the follow-up nudge. If they never opened the booking link, one polite
reminder goes out ninety minutes later with the link attached again. It cancels
itself if they reply or book in the meantime.

## Keep the enquiry

Every person who messages becomes a contact, with the treatment they asked about
and the date. When you have a quiet Tuesday, that list is who you message.
