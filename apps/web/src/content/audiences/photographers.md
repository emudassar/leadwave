## You are unreachable for eight hours at a time

A wedding, a shoot, an edit session with headphones on. Enquiries arrive in all
of them, and a photography enquiry that waits a day is usually an enquiry that
went to whoever answered first.

## The guide, automatically

Set *price*, *package*, *rates* and *booking* as keywords and send the pricing
guide as a link in a button. Tracked, so you find out how many of those enquiries
actually opened it — which is a far better measure of interest than the number of
messages you got.

## Capture both

Ask for the email in the same conversation. For a wedding enquiry, also ask for
the date — a quick-reply chip per month is enough to know immediately whether you
are even free.

## One nudge, then stop

People shopping for a photographer are comparing four or five. A single reminder
ninety minutes later, with the guide attached again, puts you back in front of
them at the moment they are deciding. A second one just annoys.

## Keep the actual conversation

Automate the guide, not the consultation. Style, mood, family logistics and what
they actually want out of the day is the part that wins the booking, and that
should obviously be you.
