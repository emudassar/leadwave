## The five questions

Every small business has about five: how much, where are you, what time do you
open, do you deliver, is it available. They arrive constantly, they arrive when
you are busiest, and the answer never changes.

## Write them once

One keyword automation per question. Set the words people actually type — the
misspellings, the Roman Urdu, the one-word messages — and the right answer goes
out before you have picked up the phone.

The whole setup takes about ten minutes and it is the last time you type those
answers.

## The part people miss

Every person who messages becomes a contact, with what they asked about and when.
Most small businesses have no list at all; after a month of this you have one,
and it is made of people who already came to you.

## What not to automate

Complaints. Put *complaint*, *refund*, *late*, *wrong* and *broken* on the
exclusion list of every automation. Someone having a bad experience who gets a
cheerful automated price list has a worse one.

## Cost

The free plan covers 1,000 messages a month, one Page, and click tracking. For a
lot of small businesses that is the whole requirement, permanently, and there is
no card involved.
