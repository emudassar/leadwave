## Interest has a half-life of about an hour

Someone watches a class clip at 9pm and messages about a trial. If the reply
comes at 10am the next day, most of that intent is gone. Automating the first
response is not about saving you work — it is about catching them while they
still mean it.

## The trial funnel

**Comment or message *trial*** → the trial pass link in a button, within seconds.

**Then ask for a number.** Messenger offers it as a one-tap chip. Numbers are
parsed and normalised to E.164 before they are stored, so your front desk gets a
clean list rather than four formats of the same number.

**Then one nudge** to anyone who never opened the pass.

## The questions that fill the rest of the day

Timings, monthly fee, is there parking, do you have a women's hour. Each of those
is one keyword automation and they never need writing again.

## Where a person is still required

Injuries, medical conditions and anything that sounds like programming advice.
Put those words on the exclusion list and let a coach answer them. An automated
reply to "I have a slipped disc, can I join" is a liability, not a lead.
