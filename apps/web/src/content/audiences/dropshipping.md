## Volume is the model

A store running ads gets more Messenger volume than almost any other kind of
business on this list, and almost all of it is three questions: how much, is
there cash on delivery, how many days.

## Three automations, most of the inbox

Set those three, with every spelling people actually use, and you have handled
the bulk of it. On Pro there is no message cap, which matters here more than
anywhere else on this page — a campaign that works should not cost you extra
per reply.

## Attach the product link

As a button, so it is tracked. At ad-campaign volume, knowing that 1,400 people
tapped through from Messenger — and that it was 900 different people — is a
number worth having next to your ad spend.

## Never automate order status

You do not have live fulfilment data in Messenger, so any automated answer about
a specific order is a guess. Put *where is my order*, *tracking*, *not delivered*
and *refund* on the exclusion list of every automation and let a person answer
them with the actual data.

This is not a limitation to work around. A confident wrong answer about someone's
parcel produces exactly the complaints and chargebacks the model cannot absorb.

## Keep the tone plain

High-volume stores get a lot of suspicion in the inbox. Short, factual answers
about price, COD and delivery convert better than enthusiasm.
