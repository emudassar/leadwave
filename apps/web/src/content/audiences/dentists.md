## The before-and-after post works

It reliably gets comments, and they are reliably the same: how much, how long
does it take, does it hurt. The post is doing its job; the follow-up is where it
falls over.

## Give a range, not a number

Set an automation per treatment — *aligners*, *whitening*, *implant* — and send an
honest range with what changes it. A range plus "the exact figure depends on the
scan" converts better than silence and far better than a number you have to walk
back in the chair.

## Attach the booking link

As a button, so it becomes a tracked short link. After a month you know which
treatment post actually fills consultation slots, rather than which one got the
most reactions.

## What must stay human

Pain, swelling, bleeding, anything urgent, and anything that reads like a
diagnosis question. Put those on the exclusion list of every automation. Same for
the AI: give it prices, timings and aftercare logistics, and nothing that could
be read as clinical advice.

## Photos

People will send photos of their teeth. An automation cannot read them and should
not pretend to — those threads land in your inbox for a person to answer, which
is the correct outcome.
