## Every enquiry arrives incomplete

"Umrah package?" "Dubai kitna hai?" You cannot answer either without dates, group
size and a budget band, so every enquiry costs you three round trips before it is
even a lead.

## Ask the qualifying questions automatically

Set an automation per destination. Instead of quoting, it acknowledges, explains
what changes a price, and asks for the three things you need. Quick-reply chips
make the group size and budget band one tap each.

By the time a human opens the thread, it is a qualified enquiry rather than two
words.

## Capture the number

Travel decisions happen on the phone. Ask for the number in the same
conversation, one tap, normalised on the way into your contacts.

## Do not quote automatically

This is the important one. Airfares and hotel rates move daily; an automated
package price is a promise you may not be able to honour, and in this business
that is a refund conversation rather than an inconvenience. Send the range if you
must, send the qualifying questions always, and let a person send the number.

## Seasonal peaks

Hajj, Umrah and summer holidays produce more enquiries in a fortnight than the
rest of the quarter. The automation does not get tired at 1am in week two, which
is the entire point.
