## You are in the room

Ninety minutes at a time, phone away, which is exactly when the messages arrive.
By the time you look, the person asking about the 7am class has booked somewhere
else.

## Three automations cover a week

**Schedule.** Keywords *timing*, *schedule*, *classes*, *batch* → the current
timetable and a booking link.

**Pricing.** Keywords *price*, *fee*, *pass*, *package* → drop-in, monthly and
class-pack pricing in one message.

**Beginners.** Keyword *beginner* → the honest answer about which class to start
with. This one converts better than any of the others, because the real question
underneath it is "will I embarrass myself".

## Workshops and teacher training

These are higher-value and slower decisions, so capture the email rather than
just sending a link. One tap, into your contacts, and you can tell them when the
next intake opens instead of hoping they remember.

## Story replies

Post the morning class to your Page story. Replies become threads with a booking
link automatically. It is the single highest-intent message a studio gets and it
usually goes unanswered until the afternoon.

## Injuries stay human

Put *injury*, *pregnant*, *surgery* and *back pain* on the exclusion list. Those
need a teacher's judgement, not a schedule link.
