## The post that did well and went nowhere

You post something that lands. Sixty comments. You reply to the first fifteen
with the link, lose the afternoon, and never get to the rest. Two of those
thirty-five would have booked.

## What to set up

**Comment → the link.** One keyword, one message, the booking page in a button.
Every person who comments gets it in under a minute, including the ones who
comment at 2am from another timezone.

**Then ask for the email.** They have the link; ask if they want the guide too.
One tap, straight into your contacts, with the post that earned it attached.

**Then nudge once.** Ninety minutes later, the people who never opened the link
get one reminder with it attached again. Cancelled automatically for anyone who
already booked.

## What you learn

Not "the post did well" — how many people it sent to the booking page, how many
different people opened it, and what share of the commenters that was. Run it
twice and you know which kind of post fills your calendar.

## Where the AI helps

The questions before a booking are always the same four: how long, how much, is
it online, what happens on the call. Write those four answers into the knowledge
base and LeadWave AI handles them in your voice, day or night — and when
somebody asks something it has not been told, it says so and leaves it for you.

## Keep the qualifying human

Automate the logistics, not the judgement. A discovery call is where you decide
whether you can help someone; nothing in this product should be making that call
for you.
