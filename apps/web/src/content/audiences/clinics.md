## Most clinic messages are admin

What time are you open, where exactly are you, how much is a consultation, do you
take walk-ins. None of that needs a doctor, and all of it currently interrupts
one.

## Automate exactly that, and no more

Three or four keyword automations cover the logistics. Add the appointment link
as a button so you can see how many people actually opened it, rather than
guessing at whether the Page is doing anything.

## The exclusion list is the most important setting

This is the one industry where the words you block matter more than the words you
answer. Put symptoms, conditions, medication names, *is it serious*, *should I
come in* and anything similar on the exclusion list of every automation. Those
messages must reach a person.

## If you turn on the AI, keep it narrow

LeadWave AI answers only from the knowledge you write. Write the logistics —
hours, fees, location, parking, what to bring — and nothing else. It will
correctly say it does not know when asked something clinical, and offer to have
someone come back to them. That is the behaviour you want, and it is the default.

## A note on records

Messages exchanged through your Page are held by LeadWave so your inbox can show
the thread. Do not invite patients to send clinical details or documents through
it; point them at whatever system you already use for that.
