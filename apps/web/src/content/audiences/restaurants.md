## The three questions

Menu, hours, do you deliver. They arrive constantly, they arrive when the kitchen
is busiest, and the answer is the same every time. Three automations covers most
of a week's messages.

## Comment "menu"

Post the special, tell people to comment *menu*, and the link goes to every one
of them privately. The public reply — "Sent it over 📩" — keeps the comments
section looking like somewhere a person works.

## Hours and delivery, on keywords

Set *open*, *timing*, *hours* and *closed* for one automation, and *deliver*,
*delivery* and *home delivery* for another. Both run at 11pm, which is when a
surprising share of them are asked.

## Booking, with a number attached

Put the booking link in a button rather than in the text. It becomes a tracked
short link, so at the end of the week you know the Friday post sent 84 people to
the booking page — not just that it got a lot of likes.

## What to leave to a person

Put *complaint*, *refund*, *cold*, *late* and *wrong order* on the exclusion list
of every automation. Someone with a bad experience getting a cheerful automated
menu link is worse than no reply at all.
