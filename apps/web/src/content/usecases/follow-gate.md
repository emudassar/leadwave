## What it does

Someone asks for the link. Before it arrives, they get a message: follow the Page
so you catch the next one, then tap below. When they tap, the rest of the
automation runs and they get what they came for.

## What it does not do

**It does not verify the follow.** Facebook gives apps no per-person follow
signal — there is no API call that answers "does this person follow this Page".
Any tool that tells you otherwise is guessing, or counting taps and calling them
follows.

LeadWave counts taps and calls them taps. Every gate unlock is recorded and
labelled **self-confirmed** everywhere it appears — in the automation's numbers,
on the contact, in exports. You will never open your dashboard and find a
follower count that turns out to be fiction.

## Why it still works

Most people who tap the button do follow, because they were about to anyway and
you gave them a reason to do it now. The gate converts because of the ask, not
because of the enforcement. Treating it honestly costs you nothing except a
number you should not have trusted.

## Set the timeout kindly

You choose how long to wait for the tap, up to 20 hours — capped so it always
lands inside Messenger's 24-hour window. Then choose what happens if they never
tap:

- **Send it anyway.** Kinder, and it converts better. Someone who wanted your
  link and got nothing remembers that.
- **Send a different message.** A softer nudge, then the link.
- **Give up quietly.** Available, rarely the right call.

## Skip your regulars

Turn on "skip for people who already engage with the Page" and contacts who have
been talking to you for months are not asked to follow you again. Nothing makes a
loyal customer feel more like a stranger.
