## Why it is here and not a separate subscription

You are already paying for something to track your Messenger links. A bio page is
the same problem — a list of links, and the question of which one people tap.
Running it in the same tool means one place to look, one click model, and one
bill.

## What is on it

- **Links**, as full-width tappable buttons, each with its own click count
- **Headings and text**, to break a long list into sections people can scan
- **An email capture block**, which lands signups in the same contacts list as
  your Messenger captures
- **Social icons**, for the accounts you actually maintain

## The same tracking, not a different one

Every link on the page becomes a tracked short link, exactly like a button in a
message. Clicks, unique clickers, share of total — the same numbers in the same
shape, so comparing "the bio page link" with "the DM button" is comparing like
with like.

## Scheduled links

On Pro, a link can be set to appear and disappear on its own. A drop that goes
live at 6pm and comes down when it sells out does not need you awake for either
moment.

## SEO, if you want it

Pro adds control over the page title, description and preview image, plus a
no-index switch for a page you only want to share by link. On the free plan the
page still works and is still indexable — you just get the defaults.
