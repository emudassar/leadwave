## Why a carousel beats a link

A link asks someone to leave the conversation, load a site, and find the thing. A
carousel puts three products in front of them where they already are, with a
price under each one and a Buy button they can tap without a page ever loading.

## The number that teaches you something

A plain link tells you 47 people clicked. A carousel tells you the hoodie got 31,
the tee got 12 and the cap got 4 — from the same 47 people. That is a
merchandising decision you can actually make: put the hoodie first next time, or
find out why the cap is not landing.

## What Messenger allows

Ten cards, three buttons per card, an 80-character title and an 80-character
subtitle. These are Facebook's limits, not ours, and the builder shows you the
real rendering as you type — so a subtitle that truncates on a phone truncates in
the preview, before four hundred people see it.

## Write the subtitle like a price tag

It is the line under the title, and it is the one people read. "Rs 4,200 · free
shipping" works. "Premium quality guaranteed" does not. Put the price, the
shipping and the scarcity in it; save the adjectives for somewhere they are not
competing with a Buy button for attention.
