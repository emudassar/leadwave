## The shape of the problem

Keyword automations are excellent at the twenty questions you predicted. They are
useless for the twenty-first, which is where most of the day goes — the
slightly-different phrasing, the follow-up question, the thing you never thought
to write a rule for.

## What the AI is allowed to do

Very little, deliberately.

It reads the message and classifies it. Greetings, small talk, emoji and abuse
are left alone — only real questions, buying intent and support issues are even
considered for a reply. Then it answers **from the knowledge you have written and
nothing else**. No web browsing. No general knowledge. If your notes do not cover
it, it says it does not know and offers to check, which is what a good employee
does.

## Your automations come first, always

Precedence is fixed and not configurable, because it should not be: a run that is
waiting for a reply claims the message first, then your keyword automations get
their turn, and only if neither fired is the AI considered at all. Nobody ever
receives two replies to one message.

## When a human takes over

Reply yourself and the AI goes quiet in that thread for 48 hours. Reply again and
the clock resets. The person on the other end gets you, not a bot talking over
you.

## What a credit is

One credit is **one reply that was actually delivered**. Reading the message,
classifying it, deciding to stay quiet, evaluating whether a goal succeeded, and a
send that failed — all free. You are charged after it lands, never before.

## The receipts

Every decision is written down, including the ones where it said nothing and why.
The overview shows you the skip breakdown as prominently as the reply count,
because "it stayed quiet 43 times and charged you nothing" is the number that
tells you whether to trust it.

## Goals, measured honestly

You can give it a goal — share a link, capture a lead, ask for a follow — and it
will work towards it when the conversation allows, never at the cost of answering
the actual question. Success is counted from what we can observe: the tracked
link was clicked, the field was saved, the button was tapped. Never from the
model saying it went well.
